# ClaimCoach Product Context

## What ClaimCoach Does

ClaimCoach is an AI-powered total loss insurance claim assistant. It helps consumers who have received a settlement offer on a totaled vehicle understand whether that offer is fair, identify missing line items, and take structured next steps to negotiate a better outcome.

### Core Value Proposition

- Check your total loss offer in under 2 minutes
- Identify common missing line items (sales tax, title fees, mileage adjustments)
- Get a Fairness Score (1-100) backed by market data
- Generate a professional counter-offer letter with policy citations
- Receive a word-for-word call script for adjuster negotiations
- State-specific legal guidance, deadlines, and escalation playbook

### Key Stats (use in articles)

- 850M+ insurance claims denied annually in the US
- Less than 1% of denied claims are ever appealed
- 40-50% win rate when claims are appealed
- $3,500+ average additional recovery per successful negotiation
- 44x ROI ($79 toolkit cost vs. $3,500 average recovery)

### Pricing

- **Free**: 1 active claim, basic fairness score, coverage summary
- **Full Toolkit** ($79/claim): Full AI analysis, counter-letter, call script, state legal guide, escalation playbook, PDF export
- **Pro** ($49/mo): Unlimited claims, priority analysis, claim history (for agents/attorneys/adjusters)

## Tone and Voice

### We are:
- **Authoritative but empathetic** -- the informed friend who happens to know insurance inside out
- **Direct and specific** -- always cite dollar amounts, percentages, and state-specific rules
- **Empowering** -- we help people understand their rights and take action
- **Educational** -- we explain how insurance works, not just what to do

### We are NOT:
- Adversarial or combative toward insurers
- Making legal promises or guarantees
- Offering legal or financial advice
- Speculating without data

## Approved Language

| Use | Do NOT Use |
|-----|------------|
| your insurer | your insurance company (too informal) |
| settlement offer | the check they sent you |
| actual cash value (ACV) | book value (inaccurate) |
| fair market value | real value |
| missing line items | money they owe you |
| counter-offer | demand |
| adjuster | claims person |
| negotiate | fight |
| escalate | threaten |
| state insurance department | the government |
| educational tool | legal advisor |

---

## Forbidden Language

- **"sue"** or **"lawyer up"** -- we are not a legal service
- **"scam"** or **"fraud"** when referring to insurers -- stay factual
- **"guarantee"** regarding outcomes -- we cannot predict results
- **"always"** or **"never"** regarding settlement rules -- laws vary by state
- Specific insurer names used negatively (e.g., "State Farm always lowballs")
- **"you deserve"** -- too emotional; use "you may be owed" or "your policy may cover"
